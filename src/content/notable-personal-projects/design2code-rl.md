[design2code-rl](https://github.com/gpahal/design2code-rl) generates multi-page websites and grades how well a coding agent rebuilds them from screenshots, with a reward between 0 and 1 that can train a model. Over 10 trials on each of 10 sites, Claude Code with Opus 4.7 averaged 0.80 against 0.98 for the oracle, and the site's design decided most of the score.
