[microgpt](https://github.com/gpahal/microgpt) is a GPT implementation and training pipeline in PyTorch, built to learn attention, RoPE and multi-GPU training. Its 124M model, pretrained from scratch in about four hours on 8 H200s for about $128, peaked at 34.6% on HellaSwag, above OpenAI's GPT-2 (29.4%) and GPT-3 (33.7%) checkpoints of the same size.
