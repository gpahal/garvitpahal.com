[durable-execution](https://github.com/gpahal/durable-execution) runs TypeScript tasks and workflows so they survive logic failures, process crashes and network errors. It's on npm as five packages, with storage for Convex and Drizzle and [documentation](https://gpahal.github.io/durable-execution).
