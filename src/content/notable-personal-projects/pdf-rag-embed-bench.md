[pdf-rag-embed-bench](https://github.com/gpahal/pdf-rag-embed-bench) measures how well text, multimodal and multi-vector embedders, sparse retrieval and reranking find the right PDF page. Embedding page images beat embedding parsed text, and the best combination reached 94.0% NDCG@5.
