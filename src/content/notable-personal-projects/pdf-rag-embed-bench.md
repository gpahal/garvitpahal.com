[pdf-rag-embed-bench](https://github.com/gpahal/pdf-rag-embed-bench) measures how well text and multimodal embedding models retrieve PDF pages: text embedders over parsed pages, multimodal embedders over page images, multi-vector embedders and combinations of them, with sparse retrieval and reranking.

Multimodal embedders beat text ones, and combining a multimodal embedder with an image multi-vector one, sparse retrieval and a reranker reached 94.0% NDCG@5.
