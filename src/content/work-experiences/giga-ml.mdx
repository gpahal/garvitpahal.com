Giga ML is an enterprise-focused AI platform that builds voice and chat AI agents designed to
automate and scale customer support and operations for large companies. Customers include DoorDash
and Cloudbeds.

I built the voice AI infrastructure at Giga, which was primarily a text- and email-based customer
support platform. The work involved solving problems such as RAG latency on the knowledge base, user
interruptions and accents, adding fallbacks for our AI providers and making every part of the voice
experience configurable.
