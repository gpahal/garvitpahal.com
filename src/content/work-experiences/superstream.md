[Superstream](https://superstream.finance/) is a protocol and a collection of SDKs for real-time money streaming on Solana. It allows anyone to continuously send money to anyone else at any interval down to a second, and companies used it for payroll and subscription payments.

The motivation was the earned wage access problem. Low-income workers sometimes face a cash crunch at the end of the month when there is a sudden expense, and have to resort to predatory loans while they wait for a payday that rarely lines up with their needs. Superstream lets workers get paid hourly, daily or at any other cadence, and lets people pay for services in smaller increments instead of monthly or yearly.

I built the streaming payment system: I designed and implemented the protocol, deployed it on Solana and wrote its SDKs. The project is [open source](https://github.com/gpahal/superstream) and documented at [superstream.finance](https://superstream.finance/).
