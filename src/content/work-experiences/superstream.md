Superstream is an open-source protocol and SDKs for real-time money streaming on Solana: anyone can pay anyone continuously, at any interval down to a second. Companies used it for payroll and subscriptions.

I built it so workers could be paid hourly or daily instead of taking predatory loans while they wait for payday. I designed the protocol, deployed it on Solana and wrote its SDKs ([source](https://github.com/gpahal/superstream)).
