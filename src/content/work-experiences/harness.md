Harness is a modern software delivery platform helping enterprises ship code easily, quickly and resiliently, now valued at $5.5 billion. I joined as one of its earliest engineers, and while I was there it grew revenue 50x and went from one product to six.

I was part of the Continuous Delivery and Pipeline teams, building the foundations every other team (CD, CI, Feature Flags and Cloud Cost Management) used to build workflow products. My main work was the design and development of the workflow execution engine under all of these products, which runs durable workflows that last weeks. I also led the move from a monolithic Java app to polyglot microservices as the product offering grew.
