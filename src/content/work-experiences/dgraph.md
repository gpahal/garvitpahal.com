Dgraph is one of the most popular open-source graph databases in the world. It is built to be distributed and scalable.

I was a founding engineer at Dgraph. I launched Ratel, which is Dgraph's data visualisation and cluster management interface. I created tooling for the ecosystem. I built JavaScript (browser and Node.js) and Python clients for Dgraph and contributed to the other clients. I also laid the groundwork for the Dgraph-as-a-service platform built on top of Kubernetes.
